Grindweh Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Grindweh is a headache diary for iPhone. In short: your entries stay on your iPhone. No own server, no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

1. Controller

2. What is stored on your iPhone

All entries are stored only locally on your device: pain intensity, pain zones and tension zones, medication, weather and, if you enable it, values from Apple Health. The widget also reads this data only locally. Your settings, your last approximate location and your Grindweh Pro status are stored locally as well.

Grindweh has no own server and no iCloud sync. I (the controller) do not receive any of your entries.

3. Weather (Apple WeatherKit)

For weather data, Grindweh queries Apple WeatherKit. Only coordinates rounded to about 1 km and the requested time period are sent to Apple, with no identifiers and no diary data. For technical reasons, Apple sees your IP address.

4. Location

Location access is optional. Grindweh only requests an approximate location, only “While Using the App”, once, with no continuous tracking. Alternatively, you can choose your location manually. With the manual location search, your search text is sent to Apple MapKit.

5. Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you enable it, Grindweh reads sleep, steps, resting heart rate, active energy, exercise minutes and workouts. Optionally, Grindweh writes the highest headache value of each day to Apple Health as “Headache”. This data stays locally on your device; nothing is sent over the network.

6. Grindweh Pro (in-app purchase)

Grindweh Pro is purchased through Apple’s in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment; I receive no payment data.

7. Reminder

The reminder is a local notification on your iPhone. There are no push notifications via a server.

8. PDF report, backup and import

Data only leaves the app when you trigger it yourself:

  • PDF report: you share it via the share sheet, with whomever and however you like.
  • Backup: Grindweh saves a JSON file to a folder you choose, then automatically every 7 days. If you choose a folder in iCloud Drive, the data is stored there.
  • Import: you can import a backup again.

9. What Grindweh does not have

No own server or backend, no iCloud sync, no analytics, no crash reports, no ads, no tracking, no advertising ID and no third-party SDKs.

10. Apple as an independent controller

Apple is itself the controller for WeatherKit, MapKit, Apple Health, in-app purchases and iCloud Drive. Apple’s Privacy Policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

11. Legal bases

Grindweh uses Apple Health and location only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the iOS settings. The purchase of Grindweh Pro serves the performance of the contract.

12. Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the GDPR, you have the right of access, rectification and erasure. Since your data is only on your device, it is in your hands: you can view, correct and delete it in the app, or delete the app. As I do not hold any of your data, I can only provide information. Questions: support@grindweh.app

You can also lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or a data protection supervisory authority in the EU.

13. Children

Grindweh is not specifically aimed at children. As I receive no data, I do not collect data from children either.

14. Changes

I may update this privacy policy when the app changes. The current version with the date shown at the top applies.

Hinweis: Grindweh ist ein Tagebuch und keine medizinische Beratung. Die App stellt keine Diagnosen und ersetzt keinen Arztbesuch. Bei starken, neuen oder ungewohnten Kopfschmerzen wende dich bitte an eine Ärztin oder einen Arzt.
